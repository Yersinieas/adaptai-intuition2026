## 🛑 The Problem
**"One-size-fits-all" interfaces are exclusionary.**
- Traditional tools are **static** and require manual setup.
- Users with motor, cognitive, or auditory impairments often feel **dependent** on assistants.
- **Cognitive Load:** Complex UIs lead to "choice paralysis" and hesitation.

---

## 💡 Our Solution: The AdaptAI Widget
**A universal "Plug-and-Play" accessibility layer.**
- **Real-time Adaptation:** Dynamically modifies any web interface.
- **Independence First:** AI intervenes only when friction is detected.
- **Multimodal Intent:** Powered by vision-language models for seamless interaction.

---

## ✨ Core Technology
- **Multimodal AI Pipeline:** Integrating **OpenCLIP** for visual intent and **BLIP-2** for interface understanding.
- **Behavioral Analytics:** Real-time tracking of "Hesitation Loops" and "Mis-tap Friction."
- **Independence Scoring:** A unique metric measuring user autonomy (Goal: 100% Independence).

---

## 🛠️ Key Features
- **Motor Support:** Automatic target enlargement on detected mis-taps.
- **Cognitive Ease:** One-click task simplification for complex workflows.
- **Auditory/Voice:** Natural language intent selection for hands-free navigation.
- **Zero-Latency:** Optimized execution loop (<50ms) for real-time responsiveness.

## 🚍 Showcase: Singapore Bus App
- **High-Utility Demo:** Integrated into a live-updating Singapore bus app.
- **Scenario:** A user hesitating to find a route triggers an AI "Simplified View" suggestion.
- **Metric Dashboard:** Real-time feedback on AI Confidence and Independence Score.

### Key Features
- **Real-time AI Intent Detection**: Predicts what the user wants to achieve (e.g., "Find Bus Route").
- **Dynamic Adaptation**: Switches between *Independent Mode* and *Assisted Mode* based on an "Independence Score."
